It's satire

The Gezine Good Boy Tracker is a joke. It's an affectionate parody made by fans of the console hacking scene. Nothing on it is a real accusation, and everything is exaggerated on purpose. "John Nintendo" is a cartoon, not a real person or a statement about any real company's behaviour.

gezine is a real security researcher. The report information shown comes only from HackerOne's public hacktivity, exactly as HackerOne publishes it, and reporting security bugs to companies is a good and normal thing to do. If you're gezine and you'd like anything changed or removed, just ask (see below) and it will be.

Takedowns and copyright

Yes, a site full of DMCA jokes has a real takedown process. If you own something on this site (a drawing, a picture, audio, text, a trademark) and want it changed, credited differently or removed, open an issue at github.com/calamityCocoa/gezinegoodboy/issues. Say what it is and where it is on the site. If it's private, say so without the details and we'll find another way to talk. We'll deal with it as quickly as we can, and we won't argue about anything that's genuinely yours.

Leaderboards and counters

  • Pick a name that isn't offensive, isn't someone else's, and isn't your real full name.
  • No cheating, scripting or spamming. The counters and leaderboards are rate limited, and scores that aren't possible are rejected automatically.
  • We can rename or remove any entry, for any reason, without warning.
  • If you want your own entry removed, ask and it will be.

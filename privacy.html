Privacy

Last updated 2 November 2026 (player codes, weekly tournament)

The short version: this is a joke website. There are no cookies, no analytics, no ads and no trackers. The only personal data it keeps is a name you choose to put on a leaderboard. Everything else is either kept on your own device or thrown away within an hour.

Who runs this site

The Gezine Good Boy Tracker is a hobby site made by calamityCocoa. To get in touch about anything on this page, including any of the requests below, open an issue at github.com/calamityCocoa/gezinegoodboy/issues. If what you want to say is private, say so in the issue (without the details) and we'll arrange another way to talk.

Rate limiting and keeping the site working are based on our legitimate interest in running a site that can't be easily abused.

What stays on your device

The site saves a few things in your browser's local storage so they're still there next time: uwu mode, whether the ASMR starts by itself and where you got up to in it, your best scores, your game settings, your Headpat Clicker progress, which Easter eggs you've found, the last name you used on a leaderboard, and your player code (a random secret that proves a leaderboard name is yours). These never leave your device unless you submit a score. Clear your browser's site data to remove them. None of this is a cookie and none of it is used to track you.

Live events are worked out from the clock in your browser; nothing is collected for them.

Working out which device you're on (for the greeting at the top) happens entirely in your browser and isn't sent anywhere, except the device type you choose to attach when submitting a score.

Other services involved

  • Vercel hosts the site and its small server functions. Like any web host, it handles your IP address and browser details to deliver the pages and may keep short-lived logs. See Vercel's privacy policy.
  • Upstash provides the database behind the leaderboards and counters (the data in the table above). See Upstash's privacy policy.
  • GitHub: the ASMR audio and the video downloads are served from GitHub Pages and GitHub Releases, so GitHub sees your IP address when your browser fetches them (only when the audio plays or you download something). See GitHub's privacy statement.
  • GitLab and GitHub: DMCA Dodge and Bootstrap Typer load the bootstrap.bas straight from the repositories it lives in (pepsipu's and other people's on GitLab, and forks on GitHub), so those sites see your IP address when you play them. See GitLab's privacy statement and GitHub's above.
  • HackerOne: our server reads gezine's public hacktivity from HackerOne. Nothing about you is sent.
  • Links to YouTube, Reddit and other sites only contact them if you click them.

The fonts are stored on this site, so nothing is loaded from Google. Some of these services are in the United States, so data they handle may be processed there under their own safeguards.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR or California's CCPA), you can ask what we hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, object to it being used, or take it with you. In practice the only thing we hold that's linked to you is a leaderboard entry, and we'll remove or rename it if you ask. You can also complain to your local data protection authority. We don't sell or share personal data, and we never will.

Children

The site isn't aimed at young children. Please don't use your real name on a leaderboard, whatever your age.

Changes

If this page changes, the date at the top changes too. The full history is public in the site's source code.
